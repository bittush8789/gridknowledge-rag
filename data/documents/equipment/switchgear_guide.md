# 33kV & 11kV Metal-Clad Switchgear Operating Guide

--- Page 1 ---
## Section 1: Switchgear Architecture and Compartmentalization
Medium-voltage metal-clad switchboards consist of four segregated grounded metal compartments:
1. Busbar Compartment: Houses the main three-phase copper busbars with heat-shrinkable insulation sleeves.
2. Circuit Breaker Compartment: Accommodates the withdrawable circuit breaker truck assembly with automatic safety shutters.
3. Cable Compartment: Contains power cable terminations, current transformers (CTs), and earth switch blades.
4. Low Voltage Instrument Compartment: Contains numerical protection relays, auxiliary relays, and control terminals.

## Section 2: Mechanical Interlocks and Racking Procedures
Switchgear incorporates mandatory safety interlocks to prevent hazardous maloperation:
1. Interlock 1: The circuit breaker cannot be racked in or out when the circuit breaker is in the CLOSED position. The breaker must be OPEN.
2. Interlock 2: The circuit breaker cannot be closed unless it is securely locked in either the TEST position or the CONNECTED (service) position.
3. Interlock 3: The earthing switch cannot be closed if the circuit breaker is in the CONNECTED position.
4. Interlock 4: The breaker truck cannot be racked into the CONNECTED position if the earthing switch is closed.

Step-by-Step Racking Sequence:
- Step A: Verify breaker status indicator displays 'OPEN'.
- Step B: Insert the manual racking handle into the truck racking port.
- Step C: Rotate clockwise to advance truck from TEST to CONNECTED position (approximately 22 complete turns). Observe mechanical position indicator.
- Step D: Ensure safety shutters open completely without sticking.
- Step E: Verify auxiliary secondary control plug is firmly latched.
