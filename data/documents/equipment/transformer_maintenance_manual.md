# Transformer Maintenance Manual

--- Page 1 ---
## Section 1: Overview and Specifications
This manual establishes the standard operating, inspection, and maintenance procedures for three-phase oil-immersed power transformers (ratings 33kV/11kV and 132kV/33kV). All field personnel must possess valid high-voltage authorization before opening transformer control cabinets or accessing the transformer bund.

Key Specifications:
- Rated Voltage: 33kV primary, 11kV secondary
- Cooling Class: ONAN / ONAF (Oil Natural Air Natural / Oil Natural Air Forced)
- Insulating Fluid: Mineral insulating oil conforming to IEC 60296
- Maximum Permissible Continuous Top-Oil Temperature: 85°C
- Maximum Permissible Continuous Hot-Spot Winding Temperature: 98°C

## Section 2: Transformer Inspection Checklist
The daily, weekly, and monthly visual inspection routine comprises:
1. Conservator Tank Oil Level: Check magnetic oil gauge (MOG). Oil level must align with the ambient temperature marking (+20°C, +30°C, or +40°C).
2. Silica Gel Breather: Verify color condition of silica gel crystals. Active gel is deep cobalt blue. If more than 60% of the canister has shifted to light pink or translucent white, replace or regenerate the charge immediately.
3. Pressure Relief Device (PRD): Inspect visual trip indicator flag. Flag must be fully depressed flush with the PRD housing.
4. Nitrogen Blanketing / Conservator Diaphragm: Check cushion pressure gauge reads between 0.5 to 2.5 PSI.
5. Tank & Radiator Bank: Inspect all gasketed flanges, drain valves, radiator headers, and sampling cocks for seepage or oil sweating.
6. Temperature Gauges: Inspect OTI (Oil Temperature Indicator) and WTI (Winding Temperature Indicator). Verify maximum drag pointer positions and alarm contact setpoints (OTI Alarm at 75°C, Trip at 85°C; WTI Alarm at 85°C, Trip at 95°C).

## Section 3: Bushing Maintenance and Testing
High-voltage (HV) condenser bushings (33kV and 132kV) must be inspected quarterly for porcelain cracks, surface contamination, and oil leaks.
- Cleaning: Clean bushing sheds using approved isopropyl alcohol solvent and lint-free wipes.
- Tan Delta & Capacitance Testing: Measure dielectric dissipation factor (tan δ) and C1/C2 capacitance at 10 kV test voltage. Tan delta must not exceed 0.007 (0.7%) at 20°C reference. A capacitance change exceeding ±5% from nameplate indicates internal layer degradation.

## Section 4: Oil Inspection and Dissolved Gas Analysis
Insulating oil quality directly governs dielectric reliability. Oil sampling must be conducted from the bottom sampling valve using clean, gas-tight 50ml glass syringes and brown glass bottles conforming to ASTM D923.

Critical Oil Test Limits:
1. Dielectric Breakdown Voltage (BDV): Minimum 30 kV for 33kV equipment; minimum 50 kV for 132kV equipment (IEC 60156).
2. Moisture Content (Karl Fischer): Maximum allowable moisture is 20 ppm (mg/kg) for 33kV service, and 15 ppm for 132kV service.
3. Total Acidity (Neutralization Value): Must not exceed 0.15 mg KOH/g. Oil with acidity > 0.40 mg KOH/g must be reclaimed or replaced.
4. Interfacial Tension (IFT): Minimum 25 mN/m at 25°C.
5. Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA) Key Limits (ppm):
   - Hydrogen (H2): < 100 ppm
   - Methane (CH4): < 120 ppm
   - Acetylene (C2H2): < 1 ppm (Any presence of C2H2 indicates active high-energy electrical arcing and mandates immediate de-energization)
   - Ethylene (C2H4): < 50 ppm
   - Ethane (C2H6): < 65 ppm
   - Carbon Monoxide (CO): < 350 ppm
