# Grid Switching Procedure and Dispatch Coordination SOP

--- Page 1 ---
## Section 1: Authority and Scope of Switching
No switching operation shall occur on any transmission line, transformer feeder, or substation busbar without explicit written authorization from the Grid Dispatch Control Officer (GDCO). All communications shall employ standard three-way repeating protocols.

--- Page 15 ---
## Section 2: Dispatch Coordination and Switching Order Protocol
1. Switching Order Preparation:
   - The field switching officer (FSO) receives the formal Switching Program document containing sequential operation steps.
2. Three-Way Communication Protocol:
   - GDCO dictates instruction: "Switching Officer Substation North, Open Circuit Breaker 33-CB-04."
   - FSO repeats word-for-word: "Substation North, Opening Circuit Breaker 33-CB-04."
   - GDCO confirms: "Instruction correct, proceed."
   - Upon completion, FSO reports: "Circuit Breaker 33-CB-04 is confirmed OPEN in all three phases."
3. Circuit Isolation Golden Rule:
   - Always open Circuit Breaker first.
   - Verify zero current flow on telemetry and ammeters.
   - Open line isolator / bus isolator.
   - Lock isolator handle in open position.
   - Close earth switch only after verifying absence of nominal voltage.
