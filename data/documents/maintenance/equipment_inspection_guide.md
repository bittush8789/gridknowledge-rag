# High-Voltage Equipment Inspection Guide

--- Page 1 ---
## Section 1: Non-Destructive Inspection Methods
This guide covers thermographic infrared inspection, ultrasound acoustic partial discharge (PD) monitoring, and mechanical vibration analysis across switchyard assets.

--- Page 16 ---
## Section 2: Thermographic Infrared Inspection Criteria
Thermographic inspections must be conducted when equipment is operating at not less than 40% of rated electrical load, preferably during peak load periods:
1. Delta-T (Temperature Difference between phases under similar load):
   - Delta-T < 5°C: Normal condition. Continue routine monitoring.
   - Delta-T 5°C to 15°C: Priority 3 anomaly (Minor hotspot). Clean and re-torque at next scheduled outage.
   - Delta-T 15°C to 30°C: Priority 2 anomaly (Major hotspot). Schedule corrective repair within 14 days.
   - Delta-T > 30°C: Priority 1 critical anomaly (Emergency hotspot). Immediate de-energization or load reduction required to prevent terminal burnout.
2. Direct Absolute Temperature Limits:
   - Copper to Copper bolted connections: Max 90°C.
   - Aluminum to Copper bimetallic transition plates: Max 80°C.
   - Cable termination lugs: Max 70°C.
