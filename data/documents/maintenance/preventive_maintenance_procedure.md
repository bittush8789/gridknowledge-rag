# Substation Preventive Maintenance Standard Procedure

--- Page 1 ---
## Section 1: Maintenance Intervals and Schedules
Preventive maintenance for high-voltage substation assets is structured on tiered chronological and condition-based cycles:
- Tier A (Monthly): Visual inspection, gauge loggings, nitrogen blanket pressure, silica gel color check, SF6 gas pressure check, battery bank float voltage log.
- Tier B (Quarterly): Thermographic infrared scanning of all primary joints and bushings under peak load; battery bank individual cell impedance testing.
- Tier C (Annual Shutdown): Insulation resistance (Megger), contact resistance (micro-ohm meter / DLRO), circuit breaker timing test, transformer dissolved gas analysis (DGA), relay calibration and secondary injection testing.

## Section 2: Contact Resistance Measurement (DLRO)
All primary electrical joints, disconnect switches, and circuit breaker main contacts must undergo digital low-resistance ohmmeter (DLRO) testing using a minimum test current of 100 Amps DC:
- 33kV Circuit Breaker Main Contact Resistance: <= 40 micro-ohms per pole.
- 11kV Vacuum Breaker Contact Resistance: <= 30 micro-ohms per pole.
- Disconnect Switch (Isolator) Jaws: <= 60 micro-ohms.
- Any joint showing > 20% variance between phases requires disassembly, de-oxidation with Scotch-Brite, application of conductive contact grease, and re-torquing to specified manufacturer Newton-meters.
