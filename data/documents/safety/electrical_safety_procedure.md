# Enterprise Electrical Safety Procedure

--- Page 1 ---
## Section 1: Fundamental Safety Rules and Minimum Approach Distances (MAD)
All personnel entering high-voltage yards must comply with mandatory Minimum Approach Distances to exposed, energized conductors. Unqualified personnel must maintain at least double the specified MAD.

Minimum Approach Distances for Qualified Electrical Workers:
- Low Voltage (up to 1 kV): 0.5 meters (Avoid contact; insulated tools required)
- Medium Voltage (11 kV): 1.5 meters minimum clearance
- Medium Voltage (33 kV): 2.0 meters minimum clearance
- High Voltage (132 kV): 3.5 meters minimum clearance
- Extra High Voltage (220 kV): 4.5 meters minimum clearance

## Section 2: Step and Touch Potential Safety
During earth fault conditions, high ground currents induce lethal potential gradients around substation structures:
1. Touch Potential Protection: Never touch ungrounded metal fences, structures, or machinery during switching or when thunder/lightning is active within 10 km.
2. Step Potential Mitigation: If an energized overhead line falls to the ground, keep feet close together and shuffle slowly away with tiny steps or hop with both feet together. Do not take normal walking strides. Maintain a minimum distance of 20 meters from any fallen wire.
3. Bonding and Earthing Mats: Substation personnel operating manual isolator handles must always stand centered on the integrated metallic equipotential operator earth mat.
