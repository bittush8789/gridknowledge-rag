# Hazardous Energy Control: Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Standard Procedure

--- Page 1 ---
## Section 1: Core Principles and Zero Energy State
The Lockout/Tagout procedure guarantees that electrical assets, stored mechanical spring energy, pneumatic pressure, and hydraulic pressure are completely isolated and brought to a Zero Energy State before any maintenance activity commences.

--- Page 8 ---
## Section 2: 8-Step High-Voltage Isolation and LOTO Sequence
Personnel must execute the following 8 sequential steps without skipping:
1. Step 1 - Preparation & Notification: Identify all energy sources (electrical primaries, secondary back-feeds, DC tripping supplies, CT/PT circuits). Notify affected grid controllers and operators.
2. Step 2 - Orderly Equipment Shutdown: Open load circuit breaker using normal SCADA or local controls.
3. Step 3 - Physical Disconnection of Energy: Open line disconnector (isolator) switches to establish a visible physical air gap.
4. Step 4 - Application of Lockout Devices & Danger Tags: Place standardized red padlocks and personalized danger tags ("DO NOT OPERATE - MEN WORKING ON LINE") on all isolator operating handles and racking shutter locks. Retain keys in the Master Lockbox.
5. Step 5 - Dissipation of Stored Energy: Discharge circuit breaker closing and opening springs (press manual discharge buttons). Bleed pneumatic or hydraulic accumulators to 0 PSI.
6. Step 6 - Verification of Zero Voltage: Test the line/equipment with an approved, calibrated high-voltage proximity detector or capacitive voltage indicator. Follow the "Live-Dead-Live" test rule: test detector on a known live source, test the isolated conductor, then re-test detector on live source.
7. Step 7 - Application of Portable Maintenance Earth (PME): Apply heavy-duty copper earthing leads (minimum 50 mm² cross-section) to all three phases and clamp firmly to the station grounding grid. Always attach ground clamp first before connecting to the conductor.
8. Step 8 - Authorization & Permit to Work (PTW): The Authorized Person hands over the PTW document and Master Lockbox key to the Work Party Leader. Only then may work proceed.
