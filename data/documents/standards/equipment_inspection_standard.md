# Substation Equipment Inspection and Compliance Standard (EIS-204)

--- Page 1 ---
## Section 1: Statutory Inspection Cadence
All grid-connected assets shall undergo formal compliance audits and standardized quality inspections according to this schedule:
1. Primary Power Transformers:
   - Visual & Physical Audit: Monthly
   - Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA): Semi-Annual (every 6 months)
   - Comprehensive Electrical Testing (Tan Delta, SFRA, Ratio, Resistance): Triennial (every 3 years)
2. Switchgear and Circuit Breakers:
   - Operating Counter and Gas Pressure Audit: Monthly
   - Dynamic Timing and Motion Analysis: Biennial (every 2 years)
   - Contact Wear and Lubrication Overhaul: Every 2,000 operations or 6 years (whichever comes first)

## Section 2: Test Instrument Calibration Requirements
All test instruments used for regulatory compliance and safety-critical testing must hold current NIST/ISO 17025 accredited calibration certificates:
- Digital Insulation Testers (Megger): Calibrated every 12 months.
- DLRO Contact Resistance Ohmmeters: Calibrated every 12 months.
- High-Voltage Proximity Detectors: Dielectric and acoustic alert certified every 6 months.
- Portable Infrared Thermographic Cameras: Radiometric calibration verified every 12 months.
